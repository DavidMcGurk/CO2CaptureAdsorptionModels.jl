## 2. Scope
Only what [haghpanah2013] needs and does not give: the heat-of-adsorption term of the column energy balance, through the dimensionless group $\sigma_i$, SI Eq. (8), with the definition of $\Delta U_{b,i}$ that it uses, Eq. (2). It supplies $\Delta H_i$ for [haghpanah2013, M1]. Also the definition of productivity, Eq. (9) and SI Eq. (39), with the bed volume and cycle time it uses, as evidence for [haghpanah2013, M5] and U9.

For information, the paper states that its model uses "the same conditions and process cycle as presented by Haghpanah et al." (p. 13653) and SI Sec. S.3 compares its productivity and energy usage with [haghpanah2013] at five Table 6 operating points (SI pp. 8–9, Table S1, Figure S1).

Not covered: everything else in the paper and SI, including the other equations and parameters of the model, the cycle boundary conditions, the other performance indicators (including the energy usage, whose vacuum-pump efficiency $\eta(p)$, Eq. 11, differs from [haghpanah2013]), the economic assessment, the optimisation, ZIF-36-FRL and the MAPLE framework.

## 5. Governing equations
None in scope. In the column energy balance (Table 2, p. 13653, nondimensional form only), $\sigma_i$ enters as $\sum_i\left[(\sigma_i - \Omega_3\bar T)\,\partial x_i/\partial\tau\right]$, the same role as $\sigma_i$ in [haghpanah2013, Eq. (4)].

## 6. Constitutive relations and correlations

### 6.1 Eq. (2): temperature dependence of $b_i$
- Page: 13652
- Verified: DM 27/11/26

$$
b_i = b_{i,0}\exp\left(\frac{-\Delta U_{b,i}}{RT}\right)
$$

Notes: "where ΔUb,i and ΔUd,i are the molar internal energy change upon adsorption for species i on site 1 and site 2, respectively" (p. 13652). $b_i$ multiplies the gas-phase concentration $c_i$ in the isotherm, Eq. (1) (p. 13652). Eq. (3) gives $d_i$ in the same form with $\Delta U_{d,i}$; it does not enter SI Eq. (8).

### 6.2 SI Eq. (8): dimensionless group $\sigma_i$
- Page: SI p. 3
- Verified: DM 27/11/26

$$
\sigma_i = \frac{\left[\dfrac{q_{s,0}}{T_0}\left(-\Delta U_{b,i} + RT_0\bar T\right)\right]}{C_{p,s} + C_{p,a}\,q_{s,0}\sum_{i=1}^{n_c} x_i}
$$

Notes: the heat-of-adsorption factor is $-\Delta U_{b,i} + RT_0\bar T$, with the site-1 internal energy change only and the local temperature $T_0\bar T$. The denominator has no density: in this paper $q$ is per kg (Table 1) and $\rho_b$ appears in $\psi$, $\Omega_1$, $\Omega_2$, $\Omega_4$ instead (SI Eqs. 1, 4, 5, 7).
