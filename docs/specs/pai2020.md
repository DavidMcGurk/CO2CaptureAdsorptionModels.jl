## 2. Scope
Only the CSS criterion of the detailed-model simulations (Sec. 2.4, p. 4) and the statement that the detailed model follows [haghpanah2013] (Sec. 2.3, p. 3). It supports the choice in [haghpanah2013, U7] to apply [estupinanperez2019, Eq. (1)] to CO2 with a 0.5 % threshold over five consecutive cycles.

Not covered: everything else in the paper and SI, including the model equations and parameters, the experiments, the machine-learning surrogates and the optimisation frameworks.

- Page: 4
- Verified: DM 27/11/26
